# Contributing to The Sociologist's Era

Every contribution that makes this curriculum 
more accurate, more accessible, or more useful 
to one more student belongs here.

## How to contribute

1. **Open an Issue** — tell us what is missing, 
   wrong, or unclear
2. **Fork and improve** — make changes and 
   submit a pull request
3. **Add a case study** — real-world examples 
   from your context
4. **Translate** — help us reach students 
   who do not read English

## What we need most

- Case studies from Global South contexts
- Plain language improvements
- Exercises tested with real students
- Connections to local research and thinkers

No credentials required. 
Your experience is your qualification.
